# 02 Booking page and confirmation email

**Problem.** The booking page is the only thing the cold email links to. It currently has a short
description; the confirmation email Google sends is generic.

**Do.**
- Description (done 2026-11-21): what the call is, what to think about, Meet, English or German.
- Booking form: Organisation name (required), "Which task takes your team the most hours?" (optional).
- Profile photo and display name on the 180DC account: a real face and name convert better than a logo.
- Confirmation: Google lets the description carry into the confirmation. Add one line there:
  "If you like, reply with a screenshot or a short description of that task; we'll look at it before
  the call."
- Check on a phone: most prospects open the email on mobile.

**Done when.** Manuel has booked a test slot from a phone as an outsider and the flow reads well.
