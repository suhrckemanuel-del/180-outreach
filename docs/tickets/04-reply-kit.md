# 04 Reply kit: what to send after "tell me more"

**Problem.** Human replies are answered by Manuel, by hand, from a draft the daily run writes. The most
common replies are predictable, and a slow or vague answer loses the warmest leads.

**Do.** A short file `tenants/<t>/replies.md` with a pattern per common reply, which the daily run uses
for its drafted answers:
- "Tell me more / what does it cost?" -> the three-step project, pricing after the call, booking link.
- "Not now, maybe in spring." -> a date to come back, logged as a touch with a due date.
- "Talk to my colleague X." -> thank, ask for the address, chain to X (counts as a warm intro).
- "Can you do it for free?" -> the branch's line on affordable-not-free (Manuel to write it).
- "Are you students?" -> yes, what that means for them (cost, speed, supervision), no apology.
- "Send me something to read." -> the offer page (ticket 03) or a one-page PDF.

Plus a one-page PDF version of ticket 03 for replies that ask for something to forward to a board.

**Done when.** The daily run's drafted answers follow these patterns and Manuel edits rather than writes.
