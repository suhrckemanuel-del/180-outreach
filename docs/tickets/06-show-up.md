# 06 Show-up: reminder and prep note

**Problem.** Booked calls get missed, especially when booked a week ahead. A no-show from a warm lead
is the most expensive loss in the funnel.

**Do.**
- Turn on Google's email reminder for the booker (appointment schedule -> booked appointment settings).
- The day before each call, the daily run drafts a one-paragraph prep note for Manuel from the
  research card and the booking form answer: who, the workflow, the two ideas from the email, what to
  ask about funding.
- After the call, `pipeline.py set --outcome proposal|no` and one line of notes, so the Sunday review
  sees call -> proposal conversion.

**Done when.** Every booked call has a prep note in the report the day before.
